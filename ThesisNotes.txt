PK Levetiracetam:
-based on Population Pharmacokinetics of Levetiracetam: A Systematic Review
- Population pharmacokinetics of levetiracetam in patients diagnosed with refractory epilepsy: A real- world study
    here only drug-resistant epilepsy considered
-k_abs usually estimated as constant
-CL usually c1*(Weight normalised)^c2*(estimated glomerular filtration rate normalised)^c3, here would also see influence of inducer drugs like VPa
-V usually v1*(Weight or Body surface area normalised)^v2
-both CL and V apparent (normalised wrt F)
-choices for Input_θ: for V parameters taken from Hernández Mitre et al.(2020), for CL bis auf c3 ähnlich wie Pigeolet et al. 
 (2007), for k_abs averaged to 3.5, since time unit days instead of hours multiply c1 and k_abs with 24
-> k_abs becomes k_abs/V, k_el becomes CL/V
-> need covariates weight, height, kidney function, calculate BSA as sqrt(weight*height/3600),
    could calculate eGFR as K*"height"/CR, maybe estimate K=0.45, "height"=50cm, CR reatine concentration (mg/dL),
    for simplicity at least at first instead just factor (1 - c3*(kidney disease yes/no))

PK Carbamazepine:
- based on Evaluation of clinical and genetic factors in the population pharmacokinetics of carbamazepine, 
    Yip, Vincent L.M. ; Pertinez, Henry ; Meng, Xiaoli ; Maggs, James L. ; Carr, Daniel F. ; Park, B. Kevin ; Marson, Anthony G. ; Pirmohamed, Munir
- age, weight etc considered but ultimately not included in model
-daily dose parameter when not yet in steady state adjusted by induction effect in first 14 days on medication
-k_abs, V as constant, for identifiability changed it to V=v1*weight
-CL = c1*(c2^received CBZ for more than 14 days yes/no) + ln(daily_dose/400)*c3, also effect of Phenytoin observed
-> potential difficulty when daily dose is 0?, minimum dose is 100-200mg, take maximum with ln(1/4), this change not considered in model
-note dose related effect controversial, see e.g. Population pharmacokinetics of carbamazepine in Singapore
    epileptic patients, Eli Chan, How Sung Lee & Swee Shan Hue
- Pharmacoepidemiologic Investigation of a Clonazepam Carbamazepine Interaction by Mixed Effect Modeling
    Using Routine Clinical Pharmacokinetic Data in Japanese Patients, Yokawa et al. uses different dose dependence, includes weight as covariate
    but no consideration of just starting medication here
-> Need covariates just whether previously received CBZ, daily dose parameter via callback
